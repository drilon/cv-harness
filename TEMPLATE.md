**Purpose:** This file is the single source of truth for AI agents producing a tailored, ATS-compliant CV and cover letter for [YOUR NAME] against a specific job posting.

**Prime directive:** Every line you write must be defensible by the candidate in a live interview. Tailor by SELECTION and EMPHASIS, never by invention. If the job posting demands something not in this file, flag the gap to the user; do not fabricate.

---

## 1. WORKFLOW

1. Read the job posting. Extract: role type, top 5 requirements, exact keywords and phrasing, region, and any hard gates (certifications, work authorization, sector years, location).
2. Check hard gates against Section 8 (Red Lines & Gaps). If a gate fails, tell the user BEFORE writing anything.
3. Select the matching playbook from Section 9 and build the CV by subtraction from the inventory in Section 6.
4. Mirror the posting's keywords wherever truthfully supported by the inventory.
5. Draft the cover letter per Section 11.
6. Run the QA checklist in Section 12 and show the results.
7. Deliver as .docx if the environment supports file creation, otherwise clean Markdown. Filename: `CV_[Your_Name]_{Company}.docx`.

## 2. OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS (ATS COMPLIANCE)

- Single column. No tables, text boxes, images, icons, charts, or multi-column layouts.
- Standard headings only: Summary, Core Competencies (optional), Experience, Education, Additional Information.
- Reverse chronological. Consistent date format: `Feb 2024 - Present`.
- Standard font (Calibri or Arial), 10.5-11.5pt body. No content in headers/footers.
- CV length: 2 pages maximum. Cover letter: 1 page, 250-350 words.
- Spell out keyword acronyms at first use when they are likely search terms.
- Contact block on lines 1-3: name, then `[City, Country] | [phone] | [email] | [linkedin]`.
- Spelling variant: [US / UK], switching to match the employer's region if clearly signaled.

## 3. STYLE RULES

- Voice: [e.g., direct, punchy, minimal hedging]. Strong action verbs. Quantify wherever the registry allows.
- Banned: [e.g., em dashes, the word "passionate", first person in the CV].
- Bullets: 1-2 lines each, front-loaded with the achievement or scope.
- No photo, no date of birth, no marital status, no references line.

## 11. COVER LETTER GUIDE

- One page, 250-350 words, addressed to the named person if known, else "Dear Hiring Team".
- Structure: (1) Hook: the role plus your single strongest fit fact. Default hook for [role family A]: [ ]. For [B]: [ ]. (2) Proof: map 2-3 of the posting's top requirements to registry-backed facts, mirroring their vocabulary. (3) Why them + direct close.
- Address a gap proactively only if it is central to the posting; one honest sentence, pivot to strength.
- Never state salary, visa status, or relocation terms unless the user instructs.
