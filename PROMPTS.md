# Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for working with your harness. Attach or paste the harness file alongside each one.

## 1. Gap check (run this first, every time)

> Here is my CV harness and a job posting. Run ONLY Steps 1-2 of the harness workflow. Report back: (a) hard gates passed or failed, (b) the posting's top 5 requirements and exact keywords, (c) which playbook from Section 9 applies, (d) every gap you would flag under Section 8. Do not draft any documents yet.

Cheap, fast, and it kills dead applications before you spend an evening on them.

## 2. Full draft

> Proceed per the harness. Deliver: (1) a tailored, ATS-compliant CV per Sections 2-3, built by selection from Section 6 using playbook [A/B/C], (2) a one-page cover letter per Section 11. Then display the completed QA checklist from Section 12 and list anything you flagged rather than included.

## 3. Hostile review (run on the output)

> Act as a skeptical interviewer who has my harness and this CV side by side. Find every claim in the CV that is stronger than the harness supports, every figure that deviates from the registry, and every framing-rule violation. Be pedantic.

## 4. Update the harness

> New fact: [describe it, with numbers]. Update the harness: add it to the correct Section 6 entry, update Section 10 if any figure changed, and add an expiry note to Section 13 if it will go stale. Show me a before/after diff of the changed lines only.

## 5. New playbook

> I am starting to target [role family]. Read Sections 5-8 and draft a new Section 9 playbook for it: which identity leads, section order, what to promote and demote, keywords to mirror, and any Section 8 red line likely to be tested by these postings.

## 6. Refresh audit (quarterly)

> Audit the harness for staleness. Check every Section 13 item, every dated or tense-sensitive claim in Sections 6 and 8 against today's date, and list every question I need to answer to bring the file current.

## 7. Cover letter only

> Using the harness and this posting, write only the cover letter per Section 11, playbook [X]. Then show which registry facts you used and which requirement each paragraph answers.

## Tips

- Always ask for the QA checklist output. The flags are the product.
- If the agent invents anything, do not fix the document. Fix the harness (usually Section 8 needs a new line), then regenerate.
- One conversation per application keeps context clean. The harness is the memory; the chat is disposable.
